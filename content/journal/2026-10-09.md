A dated, unedited entry. The journal is deliberately different from the notes: no summary, no polish, no tags, just what happened and how it felt.

Today I rebuilt the site from scratch. The old version was a handful of hand-edited HTML pages, and every change meant touching markup. The new one keeps all content in plain files and renders it in the browser, which means the part I actually care about, the writing, is the only part I have to touch.

What went well:

- Moving content into JSON and Markdown made the structure obvious
- A single command now rebuilds the index and pushes to GitHub
- The layout finally feels like mine: dark, quiet, a little mechanical

What was annoying: browsers refuse to load local files when you double-click `index.html`, so previewing needs a tiny local server. The studio starts one for me now.
